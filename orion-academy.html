Training & Development · New Company

We grow our own talent.

Orion Academy is the group's new training company, built so the person who shows up at your door is skilled, certified and held to one consistent standard.

Overview · Launching

Service quality starts with the people delivering it.

Orion Academy is a new company sitting underneath the group, created to train and develop staff across every Orion business. Rather than relying on the open market, we build skilled teams from the ground up, so quality stays consistent across home services, facilities, engineering and technology.

The Academy will run structured programmes that combine technical skills, safety, and a service mindset, with clear certification and a path to grow inside the group.
