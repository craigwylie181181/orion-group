Facility Management Services

Integrated facility management, end to end.

Orion FM keeps communities, commercial assets and estates running, with one accountable contract and a 24/7 helpdesk behind every service.

Overview

One partner for the whole asset.

Orion FM provides total facility management drawing on more than five decades of real estate experience inside the region's largest communities. We combine planned and reactive maintenance, soft services and a smart provider network into a single, accountable relationship.

Whether it is a residential community, a commercial building or a private estate, we protect asset value, keep occupants comfortable and give clients a clear, live view of performance.
