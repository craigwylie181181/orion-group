About Orion Group

One operating group, built to serve every need under a single standard.

Orion Group is structured as a family of specialist companies that share one promise: dependable service, delivered with care, backed by technology. Each company leads in its own field, and together they cover the full lifecycle of a property and the people who use it.

From the day a home is handed over to the long-term management of an entire community, Orion is the partner that keeps things running. We focus on getting the detail right, the maintenance call answered within the hour, the asset that lasts longer because it was looked after properly.
