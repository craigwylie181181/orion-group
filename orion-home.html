Home & Lifestyle Services

Everything your home needs, in one place.

Orion Home turns routine household management into something effortless. Book a service in minutes and a certified professional handles the rest.

Overview

One app, one call, one trusted team.

Orion Home is the consumer face of the group, a seamless platform that brings together the day-to-day services a household relies on. Behind it sits a smart network of vetted, certified providers and the same quality standard that runs through every Orion company.

From a deep clean before guests arrive to a car service in the parking bay, we make it simple. Tell us what you need, choose a time, and we coordinate delivery so you never chase a supplier again.
