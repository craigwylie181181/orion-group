MEP Engineering & O&M

Engineered to spec. Delivered on schedule.

Modern Construction of Building Systems engineers and executes MEP and low-current projects, then keeps them running through operation and maintenance.

Overview

A decade of MEP excellence, built into the group.

MCBS is one of the established names in MEP and low-current engineering and contracting in the market. For more than ten years our electro-mechanical teams have engineered and executed projects across hotels, malls, club houses, admin buildings, sports clubs, schools and commercial markets.

We commit to delivering every project to the highest quality, on schedule and within budget, while holding to strict health, safety and environment standards. Beyond construction, MCBS runs operation and maintenance for all kinds of facilities, managed on a fully customised SAP platform.
